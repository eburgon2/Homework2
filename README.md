# Homework2
The purpose of this project is to observe snow-water equivalent (SWE) and volumetric
streamflow(Q) to understand water balance from the previous year, particularly on April 1st of
2025. 

Based upon the Parity Plots,Q versus Historical Mean/Median, and SWE versus Historical Mean/Median, the most likely conclusion is that the reservoir will be a little low for this year, but it won’t be to the point of an anomaly or crisis. There is less streamflow to be expected, but the overall trend seems to be nearly perfectly in line with the mean. Going forward, it would be best to closely watch the reservoir levels to ensure it does not approach anomaly conditions.
